      How it works

      1. Pick a note type: romantic, a hangout, or a fun note. The paper color shows the type.
      2. Type your note or draw it by hand, then feed it into the slot. It lands in a lit box.
      3. Everyone sees the notes through the glass. Names are blurred and contacts stay hidden.
      4. Tap a note you like, insert $1, and it drops into the tray with the name and contact inside. Fun notes are anonymous: you just get the message.

      Members are approved by hand before they can use the machine. Each note can be pulled once, and you can hold up to three notes in the machine. The dollar is pretend for now: no payment is taken.
